# Agora native curriculum: units 01–06

This is the curriculum and provenance record for the native Objection-family migration. The teaching format is inherited; the sales and negotiation situations are original, fictional exercises. The reviewed JSON units remain unchanged as source records.

## Coverage

Six units contain 24 lessons and 191 semantic items: 23 lessons have eight items and u03l1 has seven after removing a redundant added match set. Every lesson has a rule with two authored immediate checks, linked scenarios, a held-back transfer, a vocabulary term, and a sequence, match, sorting, or numerical exercise. Each unit adds a fourth integration/boundary lesson; its two scenarios carry `boundary: true` so roundups can guarantee a boundary case.

## Provenance and teaching decisions

- Original lessons preserve all 72 reviewed activities. Item IDs change from `u01l1-1` to `u01-l1-1`; `originalId` records the mapping. Lesson IDs remain `u01l1` through `u06l3`. The original objective, takeaway, fieldwork, correct option, explanation, transfer status, and source IDs are retained. Each three-option choice receives a distinct, authored fourth distractor; whole options are rotated to distribute key positions. Original match and sort sets receive meaningful extra rows to use the inherited five-pair and six-row modes.
- Added explicit rule cards make the principle visible before its practice. Element lists are sets (`ordered: false`); sequence items are explicitly taught practice routines, not universal conversational laws. Every rule has authored examples, wrong tiles, and two inline checks.
- New contrast cases change a material fact: a requested demonstration after sufficient discovery; an authorized shorter onboarding option; a verified integration; stronger but still bounded evidence; changed workload; an explicit refusal; a feasible full-scope concession; completed review before a deadline; specific booking authority; a narrow new records request after sales opt-out; an approved remedy; clarified interests; a newly verified fallback; a checked reduced scope; an essential deadline; a supported opening range; and explicit package acceptance.
- Fourth lessons add proportionate discovery and information boundaries; bounded pilot evaluation; uncertainty versus confirmed mismatch; change control and handoffs; conditional reservation points; and package valuation under changed cost/capacity assumptions. These are original teaching applications of the existing source register, not newly claimed experimental findings.
- S1/S2/S10/S11 remain together on the original follow-up-question activity and its lesson foundations. Question counts are not taught as a sales formula. The source register’s correction and reply remain necessary context; no disputed effect-size claim is introduced.
- Sales foundations use S3/S4/S8 for listening, relevant questions, truthful dealing, and decision boundaries; S5/S6 for claim/evidence and material information; S7 for fabricated pressure; S9 for changes and workable agreements. N1–N6/N9 support preparation, alternatives, conditional limits, priority trades, opening evidence, and packages. E1 supports considering affected people and choice. The exact instructional routines and best-next-step keys are authored practice judgments bounded by the stated facts.
- All numerical inputs are invented exercise data, not market benchmarks. Checked calculations include 10 × 6 = 60 minutes, $600 + $200 = $800, $5,000 − $4,500 = $500, $6,600 − $4,600 = $2,000, $80,000 + $4,000 = $84,000 in the salary-minus-commute model that explicitly ignores taxes and other deductions, and $6,600 − $5,100 = $1,500. Price-overlap endpoints are inclusive only where the exercise explicitly states acceptance at the boundary.
- No legal sufficiency or business-outcome guarantee is taught. A commitment can be unauthorized and still need repair; authority must be checked before reliance. A status update is an action the seller controls, while another person’s decision remains pending until confirmed. MESOs means the set of simultaneous equivalent offers, and equivalence always identifies whose valuation and which model.
